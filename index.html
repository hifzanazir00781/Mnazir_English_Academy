Who Can Join Our English Academy?

English learning has no age limit! Whether your child is learning their first English words, you're a student preparing for board examinations, or you're a professional looking to improve your communication skills, our academy welcomes learners of all ages and skill levels.

Kids (Ages 5–12)

Build Strong Foundations

  • English Basics: Learn essential vocabulary, everyday words, and simple sentences.
  • Phonics & Pronunciation: Develop early reading skills and learn how English sounds work.
  • Confidence Building: Encourage children to speak, participate, and express themselves without fear.
  • Fun Learning Activities: Make English enjoyable through interactive exercises and guided practice.

Teenagers & Students (Ages 13–19)

Excel in English

  • Grammar Mastery: Understand tenses, sentence structure, parts of speech, and essential grammar rules.
  • School Curriculum Support: Strengthen classroom learning and improve English language skills.
  • Board Exam Preparation: Practise textbook questions, grammar exercises, essays, and important exam topics.
  • Speaking & Writing Skills: Improve fluency, comprehension, vocabulary, and written expression.

Adults & Professionals

Communicate with Confidence

  • Spoken English & Fluency: Speak naturally and confidently in everyday and professional situations.
  • Interview Preparation: Practise answering interview questions and presenting yourself professionally.
  • Business English: Develop communication skills for workplace conversations, meetings, and professional correspondence.
  • Personality & Confidence Development: Overcome hesitation and express your ideas clearly.

ABOUT THE INSTRUCTOR

Meet Sir Muhammad Nazir Awan

M.A. English, Hazara University  |  M.Ed., Allama Iqbal Open University

Sir Muhammad Nazir Awan is an experienced educator and English language instructor dedicated to helping students communicate confidently and effectively. With 25+ years of combined teaching experience, including over 15 years as a Master Trainer in Education and more than 10 years as a Spoken English Instructor, he brings extensive knowledge, practical teaching expertise, and a passion for student development.

Holding an M.A. in English from Hazara University and an M.Ed. from Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU), Sir Muhammad Nazir Awan combines strong academic foundations with practical learning techniques to make English easier to understand and use.

His mission is simple: to help every learner develop the English communication skills and self-confidence needed to succeed in academic, professional, and everyday life.

Looking Ahead: Sir Muhammad Nazir Awan is retiring from Government Service in January 2027. He is open to joining a reputable Academy or College where he can continue shaping the future of students with his extensive expertise.
